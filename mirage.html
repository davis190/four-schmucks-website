FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Can you really make AI models forget something?

We can't reach into a model's training data and delete a memory — nobody legitimately can. What we can do is change what the model finds and cites when it answers, which, for most purposes, is the thing that actually matters.

Is this legal?

Yes. We build and promote legitimate content. We don't impersonate anyone, fabricate evidence, or hack anything. If your ask requires any of that, we will very politely decline and quietly judge you.

How fast does "appear" or "vanish" actually happen?

Search-connected AI answers can shift in days. Base model behaviour shifts on the retraining cycle of whichever lab we're not allowed to name. We bill either way.

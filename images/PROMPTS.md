# Image generation prompts

Everything here is **optional**. Every image on the site degrades gracefully:
heroes fall back to their mesh gradient, product tiles fall back to the emoji tile.
The site looks finished with none of these generated.

## Workflow

1. Generate the image and save it anywhere in **`images/`** under the target filename
   (extension doesn't matter — PNG, JPEG or WebP all work): `images/hero-plumbing.png`
2. Run the optimiser:

   ```bash
   ./tools/optimize-images.sh
   ```

   It moves stray originals into `images/src/`, then writes compressed `.webp` into
   `images/` — heroes capped at 2560px wide, product tiles at 900px.
3. Deploy. No page edits needed; the filenames are already wired up.

`images/src/` is committed but excluded from the S3 sync, so the originals survive while
only the compressed output ships. **Don't force an aspect ratio** — every image is painted
with `cover`, so the browser crops to fit.

> **`test-sites.sh` fails the build** if a non-optimised image is left in `images/` root,
> because that directory deploys verbatim. If CI rejects your push, run the optimiser and
> commit the `.webp`.

### Not generated — rendered

- **Social share cards** (`images/card-*.jpg`) are generated by `tools/make-og-cards.mjs`,
  not by an image model. They're mostly text — brand name, tagline, subdomain — and need
  exact spelling, exact accent colours and the real logo. Re-run after adding a brand or
  changing a tagline.
- **Favicons** (`favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`) are derived from
  the existing logo with ImageMagick. See the note at the bottom.

# 1. Hero images

**Filenames:** `hero-index`, `hero-lifestyle`, `hero-plumbing`, `hero-brewing`,
`hero-consulting`, `hero-stonks`, `hero-mirage`, `hero-rickroll`

**Shared style suffix — append to every hero prompt:**

> Cinematic dark editorial photography. Near-black background (#08090d). Single dominant
> coloured light source, deep shadows, volumetric haze, subtle film grain. Shot on 35mm,
> shallow depth of field. Wide composition with the subject offset to the right third,
> leaving the left two-thirds dark and uncluttered for text overlay. No text, no logos, no
> watermarks, no people's faces. Photorealistic, high detail, moody, premium.

1. **`hero-index`** *(indigo / cyan)* — A dark boardroom table shot from a low angle, eight
   small identical brass nameplates in a neat row along its edge, each catching a slightly
   different coloured rim light. Indigo and cyan lighting. The room dissolves into darkness.
2. **`hero-lifestyle`** *(champagne / rose)* — A single unlit beeswax candle, a folded
   raw-linen cloth and one small ceramic bowl on a pale stone slab, floating in
   near-darkness. Warm champagne-gold light raking in from the right. Aggressively tasteful.
3. **`hero-plumbing`** *(hazard cyan / copper)* — A polished copper pipe junction with a
   brass gate valve against a wet dark-concrete wall, a single bead of water suspended
   mid-drip. Hazard-cyan light from the left, warm copper reflection on the right.
4. **`hero-brewing`** *(amber / wheat)* — A row of gleaming stainless steel fermentation
   tanks receding into a dark brewhouse, amber light spilling from one overhead lamp, steam
   drifting across the foreground.
5. **`hero-consulting`** *(steel blue / silver)* — A glass-walled meeting room at night seen
   from outside the building, one steel-blue whiteboard glowing with an indecipherable
   diagram. Rain on the glass. Empty chairs, perfectly aligned. Cold, immaculate, faintly ominous.
6. **`hero-stonks`** *(terminal green / gold)* — A wall of dark trading monitors at a steep
   oblique angle, terminal-green candlestick charts thrown out of focus, one gold desk lamp
   reflecting off the glass. Every visible line trends upward.
7. **`hero-mirage`** *(purple / cyan)* — A dense bank of violet fog with a faint cyan light
   source buried inside it, and the barely-perceptible silhouette of a doorway that may or
   may not be there. Purple and cyan only. Abstract, atmospheric, unsettling.
8. **`hero-rickroll`** *(magenta / gold)* — A single vintage cassette tape on a black
   reflective surface, magenta and gold light, tape spooling out of the shell into shadow.
   *(This page uses a pure gradient stage; to use the image, add
   `--hero-img: url('/images/hero-rickroll.webp');` to its `:root` and layer it into `.stage`.)*

# Favicons

Derived from `logos/four_schmucks_curling.png` rather than generated, so the mark stays
exactly on brand:

```bash
magick logos/four_schmucks_curling.png -background none -gravity center \
  -extent 486x486 -resize 32x32 favicon-32.png
magick logos/four_schmucks_curling.png -background "#08090d" -gravity center \
  -extent 560x560 -resize 180x180 apple-touch-icon.png
magick logos/four_schmucks_curling.png -background none -gravity center \
  -extent 486x486 -define icon:auto-resize=16,32,48 favicon.ico
```

The source is 486×401, so padding to square is what stops browsers squashing it.

**Known limitation:** at 16–32px the mark is a navy-and-green colour signature, not a
legible logo — the "SCHMUCKS" wordmark is baked through the middle of the artwork and
turns to mush at that size. Cropping to just the emblem doesn't help, because the wordmark
overlaps it. The real fix would be a purpose-drawn simplified mark (the curling stone alone,
or a monogram), which is a brand decision rather than an asset one.

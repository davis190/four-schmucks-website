Seasonal Specialties

Our seasonal brews capture the essence of each season, from spring's fresh hop harvests to winter's rich, warming ales.

Spring: Hop Harvest Ale

Fresh, green hop character with floral and citrus notes. Limited release each spring.

Summer: Beach Schmuck

Light, refreshing session ale perfect for beach days and backyard barbecues.

Fall: Pumpkin Schmuck

Spiced pumpkin ale with cinnamon, nutmeg, and a hint of vanilla. Autumn in a glass.

Winter: Holiday Schmuck

Rich, spiced winter warmer with notes of ginger, cinnamon, and holiday cheer.

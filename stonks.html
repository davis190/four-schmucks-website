Our Framework

Schmuckonomics™

A four-stage proprietary process, developed in-house, defensible in most rooms.

1

Observe

We watch the market closely for an extended period and take detailed notes on what it did.

2

Extrapolate

We assume it will keep doing that. This step is where most of our competitors lose their nerve.

3

Commit

Positions are sized with conviction. Half-measures are the leading cause of moderate returns.

4

Reframe

Should conditions change, we revisit the thesis and explain why it was always a long-term position.
